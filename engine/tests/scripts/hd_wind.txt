# Phase 2.15 wind, gusts (run with --dev-load-game=N, [graphics]hd=1, an hd_pack and
# [graphics]hd_wind=1): the map's 8-bit buffer after whole redraws with the
# wind held at several times and off equals the buffer before; the view with
# the wind held at two times (plants on screen and their sway dumped); the
# plants moved by the ticker (HD layer recomposed) against a whole redraw.
until game 30000
wait 1500
hdfx legacycheck legacy
# A gust over the player from 0 ms: still at 0 ms, leaning at 550 ms.
hdfx gust dude 0
hdfx windtime 0
wait 400
hdfx dump t0
fbshot t0
legacyshot l0
hdfx windtime 550
wait 400
hdfx dump t1
fbshot t1
legacyshot l1
hdfx windtime 825
wait 400
fbshot recomposed
refresh
fbshot redrawn
hdfx dump t2
hdfx windtime run
quit 0
