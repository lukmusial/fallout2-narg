# Phase 2.15 gunshot light (run with --dev-load-game=8, [graphics]hd=1, an
# hd_pack, [graphics]hd_shot_light=1 and hd_lights=0, so the light field holds
# nothing but the shots). The player fires a pistol at the nearest critter:
# the view before, at the shot (the shots' clock held as the round leaves the
# muzzle) and after it; then a burst (one light per round); the shots fired
# (muzzle, target, frame) and the light field once they are over.
until game 30000
wait 1500
fbshot before
shoot 8 nearest
hdfx waitshot 1
fbshot lit
hdfx shotclock run
wait 500
fbshot after
hdfx dump single
wait 1500
shoot 9 nearest secondary
hdfx waitshot 3
hdfx shotclock run
wait 500
hdfx dump burst
hdlights field after
quit 0
